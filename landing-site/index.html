Frequently asked questions

Plain answers, no marketing fog.

Visitors should be able to understand what the product does before they sign in.

What can I upload?

Supported medical documents like lab results, imaging or instrumental reports, consultation notes, discharge summaries, prescriptions, and referrals.

What does source-linked review mean?

Extracted details stay connected to the original document so you can check the surrounding context.

Can I use this for diagnosis or treatment decisions?

No. EasyHealth is for organizing and reviewing your record, not for diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

How do I start?

Use the primary CTA to create your record, then add a supported document when you're ready.
