## Why

Status color alone is not sufficient for accessible interpretation.

## What Changes

- Add decorative, variant-specific leading icons to status chips.
- Record the status-chip accessibility convention in the design system.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `status-chip-accessibility`: Non-color status cues in product UI.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- `StatusChip` and DESIGN.md only.
