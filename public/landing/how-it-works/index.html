Understanding your results

The context stays with the numbers.

A result means more when you know when it was measured, which units were used, and which reference range appeared on the report.

EasyHealth keeps those details connected to your history. Differences between laboratories and testing methods still matter when comparing results.

Questions before you start

Know what the record can and cannot do.

Do I need my entire medical history?

No. Start with one document and add others when you have them.

Will every uploaded result appear in my Health Profile?

No. Your documents and your Health Profile serve different purposes. The profile uses supported results that meet its data requirements; some information may remain available only in the document or its extracted details.

Does uploading an imaging report mean EasyHealth interprets the scan?

No. EasyHealth works with information in the written report. It does not interpret the underlying medical images.

Can I use EasyHealth to decide on treatment?

EasyHealth does not provide diagnosis or treatment recommendations. It helps you organize information that you can discuss with a healthcare professional.
