Questions

Plain answers before you begin.

EasyHealth is for organizing and reviewing a personal record, not for diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

What can I upload?

Supported medical documents such as lab results, imaging or instrumental reports, consultation notes, discharge summaries, prescriptions, and referrals.

What does source-linked review mean?

Extracted details remain connected to the original document so you can check the surrounding context.

Can EasyHealth diagnose a condition?

No. EasyHealth organizes and presents your record. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

How do I start?

Open the record entry, sign in, and add a supported document when you are ready.
