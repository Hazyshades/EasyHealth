For Clinicians

A clinic workspace for reviewing shared patient information and keeping consultations connected to their sources.

Patient-controlled access
Patients grant access to a named clinician for specific resources. Access can expire or be revoked.
Connected consultations
Keep consultation records and permitted source documents together without changing who owns the patient record.
Clinician-reviewed notes
Recording requires consent. Transcript-based note drafts remain drafts until a clinician reviews and saves them.
