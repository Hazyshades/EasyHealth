Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you begin.

What is EasyHealth?

EasyHealth is a personal health record that helps you organize medical documents, review extracted information, and follow your lab result history.

What can I upload?

Supported document types include laboratory results, written imaging and diagnostic test reports, consultation notes, discharge summaries, prescriptions, and referrals.

Does EasyHealth use AI?

Yes. EasyHealth uses AI to help extract and summarize information from medical documents. AI-generated information can be incomplete or inaccurate, so review it against the original.

Can I track results from different laboratories?

EasyHealth brings supported lab results into your biomarker history. Units, reference ranges, and testing methods can differ between laboratories, so results need to be read in context.

Does EasyHealth diagnose conditions or recommend treatment?

No. EasyHealth helps you organize and review health information. It does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.
