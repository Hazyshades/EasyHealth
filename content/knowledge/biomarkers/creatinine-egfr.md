## What it measures

Creatinine is a waste product produced by normal muscle activity. The kidneys normally filter it from the blood. eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate) is a calculated estimate of how well the kidneys filter waste, often using creatinine plus other context. The two results are related but remain separate Registry 2.0 measurement definitions.

## Aliases

Creatinine may appear as **blood creatinine** or **serum creatinine**. eGFR may appear as **estimated GFR**, **GFR**, or **eGFR**. A calculated eGFR should not be treated as the same observation as the creatinine result used in its calculation.

## Common units

The reviewed creatinine definition accepts milligrams per decilitre (mg/dL) and micromoles per litre (µmol/L). eGFR is reported in mL/min/1.73 m². Keep each value with its own unit and the reporting laboratory's reference information.

## Specimen

The creatinine definition is for **serum**. The eGFR definition has an unspecified specimen because it represents a calculated result reported by the laboratory. Do not infer a specimen or calculation method that the report does not state.

## Panel membership

Creatinine and eGFR are required members of the Registry's **Kidney panel**. A laboratory may report additional kidney measurements, and panel composition varies.

## Related measurements

Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR), blood urea nitrogen (BUN), and urea are related kidney measurements with different identities. eGFR and creatinine can appear together, but one does not replace the other.

## Interpretation factors

Interpret both results with the reporting laboratory's reference information, collection context, age, body composition, diet, hydration, recent exercise, medicines, pregnancy, and other kidney results. Creatinine can vary with muscle mass and diet; eGFR is an estimate and can be affected by the inputs and formula used. A single result cannot establish a kidney diagnosis or explain a change; a qualified healthcare professional interprets the complete picture.
